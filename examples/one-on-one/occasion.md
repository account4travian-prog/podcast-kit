## Occasion (clearly fictional example - replace with your own brief)

A community newsletter in the fictional town of Millbrook reported
that visitors using a printed festival map finished the festival
scavenger hunt faster than visitors using the festival's phone app.
The figures come from the festival committee's own sign-in sheet:
printed-map groups finished in forty-one minutes on average, app
groups in fifty-three minutes, across one weekend. The committee
itself notes the caveats: map groups were mostly returning visitors
who already knew the grounds; app groups included most first-time
visitors; nobody was assigned to a group at random; and the app
crashed twice on the Saturday afternoon. No group-by-group breakdown
has been published. This is a fictional example written for this kit;
no real study stands behind it.
