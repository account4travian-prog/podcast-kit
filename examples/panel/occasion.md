## Occasion (clearly fictional example - replace with your own brief)

A fictional community radio station, KMLL Millbrook, told its
listeners that "audiences prefer shorter episodes": in the station's
own spring survey, sixty-eight percent of respondents said they
usually finish episodes under twenty minutes, and thirty-one percent
said the same for hour-long episodes. The station's own write-up
notes the caveats: the survey was announced on air and answered by
volunteers, so it over-represents committed listeners; "usually
finish" was self-reported, never measured; and the station's short
episodes are mostly news bulletins while its long ones are mostly
interviews, so episode length and episode kind move together. No
response count by programme has been published. This is a fictional
example written for this kit; no real survey stands behind it.
