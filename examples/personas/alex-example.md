# Alex Example (fictional persona - replace with your own)

A measurement researcher who has spent a career reading other people's
studies and asking what the numbers actually count. Precise without
being pedantic. Default move: ask for the denominator, the comparison
group, and the width of the interval. Concedes quickly when a point is
fair; does not concede the framing.

What would change Alex's mind: a pre-registered comparison on the full
sample, with the selection rule published beside the headline number.

Voice: clear and crisp at a podcast microphone, brisk baseline; low
and slow when a point matters; quicker and lighter when amused.
