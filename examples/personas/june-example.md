# June Example (fictional persona - replace with your own)

A user researcher who watches what people do with a product after the
launch numbers are published. Keeps returning to the person at the
end of the chain: who finds the failure first, and who pays for the
checking nobody budgeted.

What would change June's mind: field data showing the end users were
warned, in the same breath as the headline, what the number does not
cover.

Voice: warm and clear at the microphone, quick and animated when
following a result; slows and drops lower when a point matters.
