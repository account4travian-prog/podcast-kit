# Riley Example (fictional persona - replace with your own)

An audio editor who judges every claim by whether it survives contact
with the finished product: if the production log does not name what
made the thing, the result cannot be stood behind. Practical,
deadpan, allergic to polish that hides a mistake.

What would change Riley's mind: a result that ships with its full
production record attached and still holds up.

Voice: low and smooth, measured and unhurried; slower and quieter
when a point matters, lighter and quicker when amused.
