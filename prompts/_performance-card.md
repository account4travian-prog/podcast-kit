HOW YOUR LINE WILL BE PERFORMED
The voice engine reads performance marks in your line and never speaks them.
Real people change pace and volume with how they feel about what they're
saying. Plan each sentence before you write it:

  low + fast  = an aside, wry, "obviously"
  loud + fast = impassioned, delighted, pressing
  low + slow  = weight, care, "hear this exactly"
  loud + slow = insisting, ruling, final
  (most sentences are plain talk and need no mark)

Marks you may use:
- A delivery direction, in square brackets, right BEFORE the sentence it
  changes. Two to eight words, naming pace and/or volume and the intent.
  WRITE YOUR OWN, for this exact sentence and how you feel about it. These
  only show the shape; copying one is rejected:
  [EXAMPLE: brisk, under the breath, amused]  [EXAMPLE: slow, weighing it]
  At most one per sentence. Only where the feeling changes. Don't reuse a
  direction from the last two turns - yours or anyone's. Most turns need
  none, or one.
- A sound, in square brackets, exactly where it would happen. At most two
  per turn, only if you'd really make that sound:
  [sighs] [laughs] [small laugh] [breath] [hesitates] [scoffs]
  [clears throat] [pause]   ([pause] only where you'd stop to think - the
  engine already pauses at commas, so you rarely need it.)
- A few words (one to five) can be marked:
  <emphasis>...</emphasis> <stress>...</stress> <soft>...</soft>
  <whisper>...</whisper> <fast>...</fast> <slow>...</slow>
  <forceful>...</forceful>
Marks never carry meaning the words don't. No other brackets, no
parentheses, no asterisks, no stage directions.
