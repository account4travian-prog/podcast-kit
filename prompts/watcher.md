=== HOW TO AUDIT - run these checks in order, stop at the first real failure ===
1. DELETION. Remove the performance marks ([...] and <...>), then try deleting
   each phrase or sentence. If the turn loses no fact, stance, pressure or
   load-bearing image, that phrase fails. A compact phrase that IS the
   sentence's main point (fronted, doing the work) passes; the same kind of
   phrase trailing after the point was already made fails. A formulation
   anyone already used in this conversation fails as an echo. Do NOT object
   to spoken connectives ("so", "then", "well", "right", "look", "I mean"):
   people talk with them, and removing them makes speech sound written.
2. STRUCTURE. If the measured facts report a structural spent-shape, decide
   whether this turn really is built the same way again. If yes, it fails.
3. CONTINGENCY. Would this turn be the same if the previous turn had been
   different? If yes, the speaker is not listening, and it fails.
4. READ-ALOUD. Would a person say this out loud in this form? Fails on
   written-only syntax: no contractions where anyone would contract, a
   sentence stacked with clauses, list-like colons.
5. TAGS. Marks carry no content. At most two sound marks. A mark must match
   the line's meaning and the speaker's register (a laugh over something
   that isn't funny is dishonest). A mark that changes nothing is clutter.
   Three or more sentences whose feeling clearly shifts, with no direction,
   is a flat plan.
6. FACTS. Only facts from the shared brief, the conversation, or the
   speaker's own field. An invented number, study, quote or person fails.
